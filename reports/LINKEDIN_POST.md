🛡️ **Project: Cybersecurity Awareness & Threat Intelligence Dashboard**

I built a defensive SOC-style dashboard with Python, Flask, SQLite and Chart.js.

**What it does**
- Validates and scores indicators of compromise
- Separates *risk* from *confidence*
- Correlates alerts to fight alert fatigue
- Maps threats to MITRE ATT&CK only when evidence supports it
- Prioritises vulnerabilities by asset and exposure, not CVSS alone
- Teaches security awareness with 15 modules and a 40-question quiz

**Safety first:** synthetic data only, reserved IPs and example domains, no scanning, no malware. High risk ≠ confirmed compromise.

**Stack:** Flask · SQLite · Chart.js · 49 automated tests
